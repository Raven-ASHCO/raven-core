## 1. The problem

Every indexed session this implementation starts lasts 24 hours from its
PairInit (`LAN_SESSION_LIFETIME_MS`), and a sealed envelope lives at most
`min(session end, now + 24 h)` (`raven_core::outbox::ENVELOPE_VALIDITY_MS`).
Before Phase 5 an envelope that ran out was given up (`expired`), and a send
to a contact with no session left and no way to reach him was NOT SENT. So
"one of us was away for more than a day" failed on every path: the outbox,
friend custody (a custody object is an envelope, capped at 24 h plus clock
skew) and Phase 3's renewal (which helps only while both are online).

## 2. Decision

Keep the 24 h sessions. Give each message its own **deadline**,
`MESSAGE_HORIZON_MS = 7 days` after it was written (owner decision O5-2), set
once and never extended, and separate from the session's and the envelope's
lifetime. Deliver late messages in two parts:

- **5a, park and re-pair** (no wire change): the sender's node keeps the
  message, sets up a new session when the contact can be reached again, and
  seals the message again under the same message id.
- **5b, sealed letters** (RAL1, later): when the contact cannot be reached,
  the sender seals a PairInit plus up to 16 messages to the contact's signed
  prekey and leaves the result with a mutual friend custodian (RFC1 ops
  0x04-0x06, reserved in P5.0).

Do not lengthen sessions, and do not use the hybrid ratchet for this. The
ratchet stays the separate forward-secrecy program
([`2026-10-ratchet-fs-pcs.md`](2026-10-ratchet-fs-pcs.md)); a letter can later
carry its PairInit (RAL1 reserves `pair_kind = 3`).

### 2.1 Options not taken

- **Sessions of 7 days.** One constant, but the profile has no forward
  secrecy inside a session (indexed-session waiver §4 item 1): a copy of the
  device state would read 7 days of chat both ways. Rejected.
- **The in-repo hybrid ratchet (HR v2).** Lab-only code with four blocking
  defects (FS design §4.c); that design recommends freezing it. Rejected.
- **HR3, the new ratchet.** The right fix for FS and PCS, but months away;
  even HR3 caps a session at about 7 + 7 days and still needs an asynchronous
  first message. It stays on its own track.
- **Phase 3 renewal alone.** Works only while both are online.
- **Custody holding objects longer.** A v1 custody object is an envelope tied
  to a session of at most 24 h; it cannot outlive it.

## 3. 5a: park and re-pair (built, lab only)

### 3.1 What happens to a message

1. **Written.** `raven send` seals it as today. Its deadline is
   `created_at + MESSAGE_HORIZON_MS` (`horizon::message_deadline`;
   `deadline_for` takes the staged body's or the history's `created_at`).
2. **Its envelope runs out before delivery.** With the gate live the worker
   (`settle_locally`, `finalize_vanished`), ash's reconcile and the session
   prune park it instead of giving it up
   (`raven_core::outbox::park_or_give_up_expired`): a row in
   `endpoint_parked_outbound` holds the recipient, the message id, the
   original `created_at`, the deadline, when it was parked, the session it
   came from and its send order. The outbox row goes, the outstanding row
   stays, the body stays in the protected outbound stage, and the history
   stays `queued`. The expired session's `K_root` is still pruned on time.
   The route record is kept until the deadline, so the message keeps its
   carrier choice and custody consent.
3. **A send while the contact cannot be reached and no session is left.**
   With the gate live `raven send` parks it at once (deadline now + 7 days)
   instead of NOT SENT: the parked row first, then the body staged with the
   zero sentinel binding, the route record marked `unsent`, and the outbox
   kicked. The line keeps the queued prefix and says: "queued: {who} isn't
   reachable now. When you are both on the same network, RAVEN first sets up
   a new secure connection, then sends it. It can wait until {until}." Only a
   current, unblocked contact parks (raven-node plans routes from the contact
   book only); anyone else keeps NOT SENT.
4. **The contact is back.** The worker's probe entry for the contact (one
   per contact with parked messages, on the normal backoff, kicked by an
   inbound link, a wake from sleep, a network change or `raven outbox
   retry`) runs the horizon step (`horizon::run_horizon_step`, P5.7):
   - under the contact's send lock: crash repair; a revoked identity's
     parked messages are given up (`failed`); messages past their deadline
     are given up (`expired`, "time ran out", never dialled);
   - a usable session (sendable, not letter-class, at least 10 minutes
     left) gets the parked messages at once;
   - otherwise one probe dial on each planned route that may carry a
     PairInit; once a verified offer answers, one round of the shared re-pair
     budget is counted and an ordinary synchronous PairInit V1 runs (the
     lock is never held across a dial). An unreachable contact costs no
     budget;
   - the parked messages are then sealed again into the new session, in the
     original send order, under the same message id, each copy valid until
     `min(deadline, session end, now + 24 h)`, and the copies go out on the
     immediate rescan.
   The log line has counts only: `raven-node outbox: horizon: parked=N
   resealed=M expired=K`.
5. **A send to a contact with parked messages and a session** seals the parked
   messages into that session first (`horizon::reseal_parked_before_send`),
   so they go out ahead of the new one.
6. **Deadline passed.** The message is given up as `expired` and its view
   says time ran out (with CP.6's "it never left this computer" when no
   carrier wrote it and no friend held a copy).

### 3.2 Limits

- One deadline per message, set once; a clock stepped back never extends it
  (`clock_step_back_never_extends_deadline`; harness s60c).
- At most `HORIZON_MAX_COPIES = 8` copies of one message, the first included,
  counted in the session-reset re-seal ledger, which is kept until the
  deadline.
- Re-pairing shares the one automatic re-pair budget per contact with Phase
  3's renewal: 4 rounds a day, 60 s apart (`begin_repair_attempt`); fork
  evidence or a reset loop holds it, as for renewal.
- The 32-messages-per-contact limit counts parked messages.
- Routes: any carrier the contact may use (owner decision O5-3); unverified
  contacts stay on the local network, as everywhere.

### 3.3 The receiver

A copy sealed again carries the same message id. The receiver already drops
`(peer, "in", message_id)` duplicates in its history; with the reset or the
horizon gate open (`horizon::receiver_dedup_live`) it also keeps the first
body and stamp, and it records each accepted message in `endpoint_inbound_seen`
inside the accept transaction, kept until receive time + 7 days + 1 day, so a
copy whose first instance left the 2,000-row history is still marked
"received again" instead of shown twice. An old receiver may show the rare
duplicate (an original that arrived but whose ACK was lost) twice.

### 3.4 Storage and privacy

`endpoint_parked_outbound` and `endpoint_inbound_seen` are additive tables in
the session store. In a sealed profile their device and message ids are keyed
lookups and every other value is in a sealed meta; only the inbound table has
a clear prune hour. Both are in the at-rest inventory
([`2026-10-at-rest-metadata.md`](2026-10-at-rest-metadata.md));
`METADATA_SCHEMA_VERSION` stays 4.

### 3.5 What 5a covers and what it does not

It covers "the recipient is away up to 7 days and the sender's node stays
up", on the LAN today and on any carrier once that carrier is enabled. It does
not cover "both left": then nobody can set up the new session, and that is
5b's job.

## 4. 5b: sealed letters (being built: the RAL1 codec P5.10, the prekey support P5.11, letter sessions P5.12, the custodian's letter ops P5.13, the intake and the custody client P5.14, the worker and the fetcher P5.15)

- The sender builds a **letter session** from its cached copy of the
  contact's prekey bundle: PairInit V1 (bytes unchanged), signed prekey only,
  no one-time prekey, lifetime `min(7 d, bundle expiry, certificate expiry)`
  and at least 24 h; at most 1 new letter session per contact per day.
- It seals up to 16 messages into it (an addendum to
  `ATSAM_ENDPOINT_TRANSACTION_V1` §4.1: provisional initiator indices 0-15
  on letter sessions only; ACKs still need confirmation).
- It wraps the PairInit and the envelopes in an **RAL1 letter**: X25519 to the
  recipient's signed prekey, HKDF, ChaCha20-Poly1305 with a nonce derived from
  the plaintext, signed by the sender, padded to a multiple of 512 bytes.
- It deposits the letter with each mutual custodian that supports letters
  (RFC1 `DEPOSIT_LETTER`, `RAVEN_FRIEND_CUSTODY_V1.md` §10; a custodian
  without the letter ops answers UNSUPPORTED and the base deposit of the
  direct copy is used as today). The custodian admits a letter by its header
  alone (the signer is the depositor, the recipient a pinned contact named by
  both, at most 7 d plus 5 min, 32 per pair, the objects' node, depositor and
  byte caps) and holds it opaquely in `custody_letters_v1` (P5.13: keyed and
  sealed in a sealed profile); RELEASE by the recipient and WITHDRAW by the
  depositor delete it.
- The recipient fetches letters (`FETCH_LETTERS`) without any session,
  verifies the signature before touching a secret, opens the wrap in the
  prekey actor, accepts the PairInit through the shared responder code against
  the retained bundle named by its hash, accepts the envelopes and owes the
  ACKs, and answers with a REPLY letter (PairResponse plus ACKs) sealed to the
  sender's prekey. A sender that reaches the recipient directly later replays
  the exact PairInit and gets the cached PairResponse while the responder
  still keeps it (10 minutes: the cache keeps neither name nor time on the
  disk for longer, review B-sealed-stores-5), and after that the one its
  letter row keeps (P5.15); without a PairResponse it falls back to a fresh
  re-pair. The responder's PairResponse to a letter stays valid until
  the PairInit's end, and it keeps at most 8 live letter sessions per
  initiator (`LETTER_CLAIM_LIMIT` keeps the letter at the custodian).
- Letter sessions are ACK-only at both ends: a peer-initiated session longer
  than 24 h is never a target for new chat, so the 24 h exposure limit for
  chat stays (`session_is_letter_class`, built in P5.4/P5.7). Both ends also
  mark a letter session in the session store (`indexed_session_letter`,
  P5.12: role 1 with the exact PairInit at the sender, role 2 at the
  recipient), which makes it letter-class whatever its length and lets the
  sender's provisional session seal indices 0-15.
- The custodian sees neither the PairInit nor the message ids (lost-state
  design §19, PairInit §7).
- **Intake and the custody client (P5.14).** `async_letter::intake` runs the
  RAL1 verification order (the signature before the prekey actor is asked
  anything), the PairInit through the shared responder core, then each
  envelope through the same store calls as a fetched object (relayed ingest
  steps 4-9; their ACKs stay valid until the session ends, since they travel
  back in a REPLY letter). The REPLY is owed durably: the responder's letter
  row keeps the exact PairResponse (sealed in a sealed profile) and the ACKs
  are in the owed-ACK store; a later letter of the same session, or the same
  letter fetched again, skips the core. A REPLY confirms the initiator's
  letter session and settles its ACKs; one for no letter session of ours is
  refused (`LETTER_BINDING`). `custody_client` adds `deposit_letters`,
  `fetch_letters_round` (a letter is released only on the dial after its
  intake committed; `LETTER_CLAIM_LIMIT`, `LETTER_STORE` and an envelope to
  retry keep it) and `withdraw_letters`, and one in-memory capability cache
  per custodian (`{letters, handoff}`, 24 h, learned from `UNSUPPORTED` or any
  served answer) that the handoff ops reuse. Fetch targets now include the
  custodians a pinned contact announced to this node, with no session
  needed. The worker and the fetcher start using them in P5.15.
- **The worker and the fetcher (P5.15).** The worker's custody phase runs the
  letter step first, only with custodians the capability cache knows to
  serve letters: under the contact's send lock (store work only, never
  across a dial) the parked messages and the direct copies it could not
  deliver move into the open letter session (one with a day left, room
  below index 16 and a PairInit to the bundle cached now; else a new one, at
  most one a day): each is parked, then sealed again under its message id in
  send order (the horizon's re-seal and copy cap), and the new copies are
  sealed into RAL1 letters (`horizon::letters::build_letters`). The same
  letter bytes go to each such custodian; owed ACKs of letter sessions this
  node accepted travel in REPLY letters (the kept PairResponse and up to 32
  ACKs, `build_replies`), never the base deposit. `letter_deposits.json`
  (at-rest kind 69, sealed in a sealed profile) keeps each letter, its
  custodians and the last refusal per custodian. A letter every custodian
  refused stays `unplaced` with its messages in the letter session (nothing
  is lost); placement is tried again after 1 h doubling to 24 h (never past
  the letter's expiry), and at once after this node's RELEASE at that
  custodian or a fresh answer that it serves letters. Its messages show
  `WAITING_FOR_CARRIER`, `CARRIER_FULL` (every custodian refused for space)
  or `NO_SHARED_CARRIER` (no custodian listed by both sides), and `IN_CUSTODY`
  once one holds it. A message in a provisional letter session never goes
  out directly as it is: when the contact has routes, the session's exact
  PairInit is replayed; its PairResponse confirms the session and the
  messages go out in the same attempt; no PairResponse (an older peer, a
  rotated bundle) parks them again for the horizon's synchronous re-pair
  (still under the copy cap) and gives the letter session up. Letters whose
  messages all settled, or whose session was given up, are withdrawn. The
  fetcher runs FETCH_LETTERS at every fetch target with or without a live
  session, kicks the outbox of the contacts whose letters it took and
  withdraws settled letters at that custodian. Custodians without the letter
  ops get the base deposit, and nothing is moved.

## 5. Security

- **5a forward secrecy:** unchanged. Every copy lives in an ordinary 24 h
  session; the body waits in the protected outbound stage, as a queued
  message always did. What changes is how long a message may wait to be sent
  (7 days instead of about 1).
- **5a duplicates:** bounded by the 8-copy cap and the receiver dedup.
- **5a sessions:** more PairInits for contacts who come and go, within the
  shared re-pair budget (4 a day per contact) and the responder's claim caps.
- **Letters (5b), chain 0:** anyone holding the recipient's retained prekey
  generation (until bundle expiry + 7 days, about 37 days at worst) and the
  letter bytes can read it, the same window as today's PairInit, with more
  content inside it. One-time prekeys (FS design D12) would shrink it.
- **Letters (5b), wrap:** classical X25519 only, as the Noise XX links;
  content keeps ML-KEM protection inside the session.
- **Letters (5b), replay:** the custodian deduplicates by digest; an exact
  PairInit is idempotent, a conflicting one is refused; claims are kept until
  PairInit expiry; at most 8 letter claims per initiator.

## 6. Compatibility

| Sender | Receiver | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 5a on | any | Only ordinary PairInit V1 and RVN1 messages on the wire. A new receiver drops a re-sealed copy it already took; an old one may show it twice (rare: the first copy arrived but its ACK was lost). |
| 5a off | any | Byte-identical to before: an expired message is given up, a send with no session and no route is NOT SENT. |
| 5b on | v1 custodian | No letter is deposited; the v1 deposit of the direct copy is used. |

## 7. Tests

- Unit and store tests per step (P5.3-P5.8), in `raven-core`, `raven-node`
  and `ash`, with the lab clock per thread (`raven_core::clock`).
- Real processes (`scripts/lan_fault_matrix.py`, `RAVEN_FAULT_HORIZON=1`;
  each scenario its own lab clock file, shared by both profiles):
  - s60 `offline_three_days_sender_online`: two messages queued, then
    parked after 25 h, a third parked when sent at 72 h; Bob back: all 3
    once and in order within 60 s, delivered, `resealed=3`;
  - s60b `offline_past_deadline`: Bob back after 7 days and 1 hour: both
    `expired` with the time-ran-out text, nothing reaches Bob, no PairInit;
  - s60c `clock_back_while_parked`: the clock 2 days back, then 3 ahead: the
    deadlines do not move, each message delivered once, sealed again once;
  - s94 `session_expired_while_apart` (cutoff study; also in the default
    list): both away 25 h, three sends; gate closed: NOT SENT with CP.6's
    "secure connection ... ended" text and no stray `queued`; gate open: all
    3 delivered once, in order, after the re-pair.
- Letters (5b, P5.16; `RAVEN_FAULT_LETTER=1`, which opens the letter gate
  for every node; Alice, Bob and Carol, the custodian both list, on one lab
  clock):
  - s61 `offline_three_days_via_friend_letter` (this design's s51): Bob away,
    Alice sends, Carol holds one letter; Alice away, Bob back at 72 h takes
    it (once) and leaves his REPLY; Alice back at 73 h: delivered, and Carol
    holds no letter;
  - s61b `letter_then_direct_return` (s51b): Bob back at 48 h while Alice is
    online: the letter session is completed directly (her kept PairInit
    replayed), one inbox row, and her fetcher withdraws the letter;
  - s62 `letter_base_ops_custodian` (s52): Carol without the letter ops
    (`RAVEN_LAB_CUSTODY_BASE_OPS_ONLY=1`): no letter, the base deposit, s30's
    timing;
  - s75 `letter_mule_two_rooms`, s76 `letter_week` (Bob back at 6 d 23 h:
    delivered), s76b `letter_week_ran_out` (7 d 1 h: gone, expired with the
    time on this computer's clock), s76c `twenty_in_one_day` (16 in the
    day's letter session, 4 parked, the next day's session takes them; all
    20 once and in order) from the cutoff study;
  - s30 with the letter gate on (its message may go as a letter).
- Regression: s01-s14 and s30 with every new gate off and with
  `RAVEN_FAULT_HORIZON=1`; the RSR1 scenarios s05b, s05d and s22 with reset
