Raven terminal app — design mockup

How the full-screen app (raven or ash with no arguments) looks and moves. The motion is honest: every animation reflects something Raven really does (a status check, a message travelling, a signed receipt coming back, a retry). The logo is the website's mark drawn with Braille dots; terminals without Braille show R A V E N. Plan: docs/roadmap/plans/tui-followup-plan.json, step UXF.10.
